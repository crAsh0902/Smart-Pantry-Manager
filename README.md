SMART PANTRY MANAGER.
The Smart Pantry Manager is an Android application designed to help users keep track of the ingredients they currently have at home.

WHY CHOOSE SQLite?
SQLite is built into Android which makes it very easy, compared to trying to connect an account/server and maintain internet connection.
MySQL/SQLite was used in my 2nd-year of Richfield, so it's a very familiar.

APP INSTRUCTIONS:
1. Open the app and navigate through each screen to read the messages.
2. The easiest recipe is Homemade Tomato Chips which consists of 2 ingredients, so tap the "+" button.
3. Under Ingredient Name "Tomato", under Ingredient Quantity "2", and under Ingredient Unit "whole" (Expiry Date is optional).
4. Tomato - 2 - whole will appear in the Pantry, the tap the "+" again.
5. Under Ingredient Name "Salt", under Ingredient Quantity "1", and under Ingredient Unit "tsp".
6. Salt - 1 - tsp will appear in the Pantry.
7. Navigate to the Suggested Recipes screen, and you can now access Homemade Tomato Chips.
8. Tap on the recipe, and read through the required ingredients and the instructions to cook it.
